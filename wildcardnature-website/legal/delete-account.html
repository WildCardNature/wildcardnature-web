Delete Your WildCard Nature Account

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

This page explains how to delete your WildCard Nature account and its data. WildCard Nature is published on Google Play by Jason Satz.

1. Delete Your Account in the App

Deleting in the app is the fastest option and takes effect right away. It works the same way whether you signed in with Google or play as a guest.

  1. Open WildCard Nature. If you signed in with Google, make sure you are still signed in.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  4. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  5. Review the list of what will be deleted, then confirm.

Want to keep your cards? Export a backup file under Settings before you delete your account. Deletion cannot be undone, and we cannot recover your cards or progress afterward. A backup made as a guest can be restored into any account later.

2. Guest Accounts

If you tapped Continue as guest instead of signing in with Google, your account is identified only by a random guest ID, created with Google Firebase Anonymous Authentication. We never receive a name or email address for a guest, and the guest ID is stored only on your device.

  • Want to keep your guest progress instead? Use Keep your progress in the app to sign in with Google. Your guest's synced progress is moved into your Google account, and the guest record and guest ID are deleted. After that, your account is a Google account and section 3 applies to it. You can also export a backup file under Settings; backups made as a guest can be restored into any account.
  • Delete in the app before you uninstall. Use the steps in section 1. Deleting a guest account removes the same data as for a Google account (see section 4), with no Google step.
  • If you can no longer open the app (for example, you already uninstalled it or cleared its data), email us at tex@discvault.us. Please understand that we cannot identify guest data without the guest ID, which was stored only on your device, so in most cases we will not be able to find or delete it. The progress record that remains is linked only to that random ID, not to your name, email address, or any other detail we could match to you.

3. Request Deletion Without the App (Google Accounts)

If you signed in with Google and no longer have the app, you can ask us to delete your account by email:

  1. Send an email to tex@discvault.us from the Google account email address you used to sign in to WildCard Nature. This lets us confirm that the request comes from the account owner.
  2. Use the subject line "WildCard Nature account deletion" and include the email address of that Google account in the message.
  3. We will confirm when your account has been deleted. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Example message:

Subject: WildCard Nature account deletion Please delete my WildCard Nature account and associated data. Google account email: [your Google account email]

If we cannot match the request to an account, we may ask you to verify ownership before deleting anything. We will only use the information you send for this request.

4. What Gets Deleted

  • Synced game progress on our server: XP, level, streaks, achievements, Field Guide progress, in-game Field Tokens, and daily generation usage. For guests, this is the record stored under your guest ID.
  • Purchased generations and purchase history on our server (Google accounts only): any unused generations from generation packs, and the record of your purchases. Unused purchased generations are lost and are not refunded, so use them before you delete your account.
  • Your leaderboard entry: display name, avatar choice, XP, and level.
  • Your anonymous Firebase user, which is used to access the leaderboard. For guests, this is also your guest ID.
  • The app's access to your Google account, which is revoked (Google sign-in only).
  • Data stored on your device, when you delete in the app: cards, generated artwork, photos, rounded locations, profile, your list of hidden players, and sign-in tokens. If you request deletion by email instead, uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove this data.

5. What Is Kept, and for How Long

  • Weekly discovery records: the per-user flag that stops a "Species of the Week" discovery from being counted twice. It expires automatically at the end of that week, within 7 days. Regional discovery totals are anonymous counts and also expire weekly.
  • Daily generation counters: the per-day count of cards created, stored with your account ID (Google account ID or guest ID) and your device's local date to enforce the daily free limit. They are not deleted early, and are deleted automatically 3 days after they are created. For guests, a separate per-network counter is keyed by a one-way hash of the IP address combined with a secret value that changes daily (the raw IP address is never stored); it is also deleted automatically after 3 days.
  • App Check totals: daily counts of requests that passed or failed our app integrity check. They contain no account, device, or IP information and are deleted automatically after 14 days.
  • Rate-limit counters: keyed by IP address, not by account. They expire automatically within one hour.
  • Content reports you sent about AI-generated artwork or other players: kept for up to 90 days for safety and moderation review, then deleted automatically. They are not deleted early when you delete your account. A report identifies its sender only by a one-way hash of the Google account ID or guest ID (or, if sign-in could not be verified, of the IP address combined with a secret value that changes daily). Reports never contain your name, email address, or raw IP address. Reports that other players sent about your leaderboard entry contain that entry's ID and are kept on the same 90-day schedule.
  • Purchase duplicate-protection records: one record per generation pack purchase, keyed by the Google Play transaction ID, so that a purchase or refund is never applied twice. It contains the product, the number of generations, whether the purchase was credited or refunded, and a one-way hash of your Google account ID (never the ID itself, your name, or your email address). It is deleted automatically 400 days after the purchase or refund was last processed.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat: Google Play (which takes payment) and RevenueCat (which verifies purchases for us) keep records of completed purchases under their own privacy policies and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations. Deleting your account does not delete them. To ask us to request deletion of your RevenueCat customer record, email tex@discvault.us from the Google account you bought with.
  • Regional species lists cached by our server: shared reference data with no personal information. These are not deleted.
  • Data held by service providers: photos already sent to Stability AI for stylization are kept under Stability AI's own retention terms. Region names sent to Anthropic are deleted by Anthropic within 30 days under its API terms. See our Privacy Policy for details and links.
  • Backup files you exported yourself: these are stored wherever you saved them, outside our control. Delete them yourself if you no longer want them.
